# R7 — Visual / aesthetic design review (iChirp `main` @ 53bc2cc6)

## Overall assessment

The warm light palette, the Create card and recipe tiles, the Transcript reading view (speaker inks, tinted current
paragraph, coral player) and the formatted document card already look polished and recognisably "Parakeet". The
tokens are applied with real discipline: one stray literal colour in ChirpUI, 78 contrast pairs under test, and the
custom-built screens hold up well in dark mode. The weak spots come from two places. The first is stock
SwiftUI/UIKit parts: segmented controls, the Recipes `List`, text-field placeholders and toggles, which ignore the warm
palette, Dynamic Type or both. The second is one shared layout helper, `SettingsRow`, which centres its rows and
leaves chevrons on their own line even at the default text size. The opaque cream pixel-art also puts a bright box on
every dark-mode launch. Several things that are one concept are drawn two or three ways: generated-document rows,
bottom action bars, Summary icons, placeholders and card primitives. The generated-document screen also titles every
document "Summary" or "SOAP note" without saying what it was made from. Accessibility text sizes mostly reflow well
(Create tiles, Library rows, transcript), but fixed-size glyphs, the brand mark and the system controls stay small
next to the large text. Nothing reads as fake or dishonest; what remains is consistency and the last 10% of layout
detail.

  `iChirp`) with `-derivedDataPath …/scratchpad/review/dd CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`. The app was installed on the

  normal launch path.

  - `M4ScreenTourUITests` stopped at line 193 for the same reason: saving the cloud key failed.
- **How the screens were reached.** Taps through the iOS Simulator MCP, `xcrun simctl io … screenshot` for the

- **Dictating and Meeting.** There is no microphone path in the simulator, so these two are the app's own idle-state

- **First run.** These screens came from a temporary clean simulator, which I created and then deleted.
- **Not captured.** The true "Your library is empty" screen: the second temporary device needed a simulator-panel

## Findings, most severe first

### R7-1 [high] [visual] — Settings rows fall back to a centred, orphaned layout at the default text size

- **Where:**
  - Screenshots: settings-light.png (Stop mode, Keep meeting audio), settings-light-privacy.png (Mac companion),
    settings-speech-engines-light.png (Live text, Transcripts, and the shrunken PARAKEET → Model version card),
    settings-dark.png, settings-dark-2.png, settings-axl.png, settings-axl-2.png.
  - Code: `App/Sources/Screens/Settings/SettingsComponents.swift:61-73` (the `ViewThatFits` fallback `VStack`) and
    `:19-33` (the group's card `VStack`, which has no `maxWidth`).
- **Problem:** `ViewThatFits` measures a row's caption at its unwrapped width. Any row whose caption runs past one
  line therefore fails the side-by-side layout and falls back to the stacked `VStack`. That `VStack` has no
  `maxWidth`, so it is centred in the card. At the default size this produces four visible faults:
  - Titles in the same card start at different x positions: 12–48 pt indents.
  - The value or picker drops to its own line under the caption.
  - The chevron on "Mac companion" and "Speech engines" is left alone on a third line.
  - A single-row group shrinks to its content: the Model version card ends about 60 pt short of every other card.

  At AX size, "Dictation trigger" is centred and "Keep dictation audio" is indented, while "Back Tap" in the same card
  stays side by side.
- **Evidence:**
  - Title left edges, in display px: 88 for most rows; 116 for Stop mode and Keep meeting audio; 197 and 193 for
    Live text and Transcripts.
  - Card right edges: 725 for PARAKEET against 863 for every other card.
- **Fix:**
  1. Add `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity, alignment: .leading)` to the fallback `VStack`, or after the `ViewThatFits`.
  2. Keep the side-by-side layout when the trailing view is compact. Give the caption `layoutPriority(1)` and let it
     wrap, use `trailing.fixedSize()`, and stack only at `isAccessibilitySize` or when trailing is wide.
  3. Pin the chevron of navigation rows to the trailing edge and never stack it.
  4. Add `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)` to the card `VStack` at line 19.

  **Verify:** retake settings-light.png, settings-light-privacy.png, settings-speech-engines-light.png and
  settings-axl.png. Every title should share one x and every card should span the full width.
- **Confidence:** CONFIRMED

### R7-2 [high] [dark-mode] — Opaque cream pixel-art makes a bright box on every dark-mode launch and in About

- **Where:**
  - Screenshots: launch-dark-2.png (crops/launch-dark-2-small.png), settings-dark-3.png (crops/about-dark.png).
  - Asset: `App/Resources/Assets.xcassets/ParakeetArt.imageset/parakeet-art.png`, 1024×894 with no alpha channel
    and a baked cream `#FCF9F5` field.
  - `App/Resources/Assets.xcassets/LaunchBackground.colorset`: the dark value is `#15120F`.
  - `project.yml:53-55`: the `UILaunchScreen` image and colour.
  - `App/Sources/Screens/Settings/AboutSection.swift:51-61`.
- **Problem:** In dark mode the launch background is the warm near-black, but the PNG carries its own cream
  background. The launch screen therefore shows a hard-edged rectangle of about 340×300 pt, which then cross-fades into
  the dark app. That glare flash happens on every cold launch at night, which is exactly what the owner-approved dark
  palette (F6) set out to avoid. About shows the same box. Its comment says the art sits "on its own cream plate so it
  reads the same in light and dark mode", but `Color("LaunchBackground")` is dark in dark mode, so the "plate" is
  dark and the box sits on it.
- **Evidence:** `sips` reports `hasAlpha: no`. Sampled `#FCF9F5` inside the art against `#1B1712`/`#15120F` around
  it: about 17:1.
- **Fix:** Re-export the art with a transparent background and add a dark-appearance variant in
  ParakeetArt.imageset. If the cream plate is wanted in About, draw it on purpose there: a rounded plate at
  `Tokens.Radius.l` with padding, behind a transparent image. **Verify:** set the simulator to dark, cold-launch and
  capture the first frames, then retake settings-dark-3.png.
- **Confidence:** CONFIRMED

### R7-3 [medium] [a11y] — Text-field placeholders are 1.7:1 (light) and 2.5:1 (dark), with two placeholder treatments

- **Where:**
  - Screenshots: library-light.png / library-dark.png (search), ask-light.png / ask-dark.png (input),
    transform-sheet-light.png ("Notes for the model"), settings-add-model-editor-light.png ("Context window").
  - System placeholders: `LibraryScreen.swift:126`, `AskView.swift:176`, `TransformSheet.swift:285`,
    `PasteLinkSheet.swift:118`, `CreateSheet.swift:262`, `ModelsProviderEditor.swift:95-185`.
  - Custom placeholders: `TranscriptNotesSheet.swift:93-95`, `TextItemSheet.swift:104-106`.
- **Problem:** Every `TextField` uses the system placeholder colour: `#C5C5C7` on white is 1.72:1, and `#5C595A` on

  `secondary` under F89 (5.3:1 light, 6.5:1 dark). Library search and Ask are main flows, and the placeholder is

  2. Use it at all five call sites.
  3. Add "secondary placeholder on surface" to `ContrastTests`.

  **Verify:** retake library and ask in both modes.
- **Confidence:** CONFIRMED

### R7-4 [medium] [a11y] — Segmented controls, glyphs and the brand mark ignore Dynamic Type

- **Where:**
  - Screenshots:
    - document-axl.png: Formatted | Edit stays about 13 pt next to AX-size text.
    - render-meeting-idle-ax2-dark.png: the Notes | Live transcript control stays small at AX2.
    - transcript-axl.png: 19 pt bar icons, and "Transform" runs to the screen edge.
    - capture-axl.png: the 27 pt mark sits next to a doubled wordmark.
  - Segmented pickers: `SettingsScreen.swift:222`, `SpeechEnginesScreen.swift:170`, `TransformComponents.swift:438`,
    `MeetingScreen.swift:242`, `CreateSheet.swift:351`, `StructureModelsSettingsGroup.swift:44`.
  - Fixed glyph sizes (about 55 `.font(.system(size:))` calls in total), for example `TranscriptScreen.swift:608`,
    `DocumentScreen.swift:427`, `TransformComponents.swift:108` and `:316` (9 pt chevrons), `CreateSheet.swift:324`
    and `:392`, `SettingsScreen.swift:100` and `:237`, `SettingsComponents.swift:117`, and `CaptureScreen.swift:178-179`
    (the mark).
- **Problem:** spec/04-ui.md says every text follows Dynamic Type. The custom text does (`chirpFont` uses
  `@ScaledMetric`), but `UISegmentedControl` titles don't scale and the fixed glyph sizes stay fixed. At accessibility
  sizes, chevrons, toolbar icons and the segmented controls look tiny next to the scaled labels.
- **Fix:**
  - Build a `ChirpSegmentedControl`: capsule segments with `chirpFont` and token fills. This also fixes part of R7-5.
  - Replace the fixed glyph sizes with text-style fonts, `.imageScale` or `@ScaledMetric`.
  - Scale the mark's frame with `@ScaledMetric`.

  **Verify:** retake document-axl.png, transcript-axl.png and capture-axl.png.
- **Confidence:** CONFIRMED
