## Why this matters

Nine independent reviewers read the whole repository on 2026-10-01 (reports in
[`docs/reviews/2026-10-01-full-review/`](../reviews/2026-10-01-full-review/)). The core is careful work, but the
reviews converge on a few real risks to a physician's notes: the language model and the exports receive the raw
engine words instead of the text the person reviewed (three reviewers independently), a document cut off at the
model's output limit is saved as complete (two reviewers), a dictation started from a Clinical flow is stored
Personal until later, meeting and dictation audio can be silently lost after a disk-full or a kill, and several
screens say something the code does not do ("Recording" while paused, local-only Copy that is not).
